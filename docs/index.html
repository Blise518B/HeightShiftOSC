VRChat OSC · avatar height

Change your height in VRChat, smoothly.

HeightShift reads your avatar's height and shows it next to the one you want, on a size picture from 1 cm to 100 m. Pick a height, then go there instantly or grow into it over seconds, minutes or hours.

What it does

Pick a height, then go.

See both heights

You in front, the target as a shadow behind, standing on a log scale with their eyes at the height — VRChat's height is eye height. The ratio is written out.

Pick, then go

Click or drag along the scale, pull a head, type 50 m or load a saved height. Nothing is sent until Go instant or Go timed.

Timed transitions

Over seconds, minutes or hours, at even speed or even growth, with a graph and Pause / Stop. 30 smooth updates a second.

Grow while held

Hold numpad + or − in VRChat to grow or shrink smoothly, or nudge by 1, 10 or 100 cm. Your keys, your speed.

Keeps your height

Switch avatars and it puts back the height you had. Six saved heights — make one go instant and 1 cm is one click away.

Reads before it writes

On start it only reads. Every height it sends is checked: up to 100 m, or 1000 m once you unlock it.

A menu on your avatar

Install

Three steps, one of them optional

01

Download and run

HeightShift.exe — one file, nothing to install. New versions install themselves with one click.

02

Turn on OSC

In VRChat: Action Menu → Options → OSC → Enabled. HeightShift finds VRChat by itself.

03

Add the menu

Optional: the VRChat menu prefab, to change your height without leaving VR.
